# Welcome to My Developer Blog!

Hello world! Welcome to my personal developer blog and portfolio hosted directly on **GitHub Pages**. 

I created this platform to share my thoughts on software architecture, share practical coding tutorials, rant about engineering anti-patterns, and showcase open-source projects.

## What You'll Find Here

1. **Dev Musings (`#devMusings`)**: Engineering insights, architectural patterns, and thoughts on technology trends.
2. **Dev Rants (`#devRants`)**: Constructive critiques on web bloat, modern tooling complexity, and tech industry norms.
3. **Projects Showcase**: Interactive demos and descriptions of tools and libraries I've built.
4. **Dev Portfolio**: My professional experience, skills, and background.
